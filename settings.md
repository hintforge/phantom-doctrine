# Phantom Doctrine -- Settings Reference

**status:** research-integrated
**last_reconciled:** 2026-08-18

Organized by the game's actual in-game Options tabs (confirmed at P1): **Video, Audio, Interface, Gameplay.**

## Video

Mode (window / borderless / fullscreen), Resolution, Gamma, V-Sync, quality Preset (Low - Very High), Anti-Aliasing.

**Difficulty-relevant tip:** most missions are night-urban maps -- raising Gamma brightens them, aiding the spotting of guards/loot.

**Known bug:** the game can launch at a broken resolution. Fix by editing `GameUserSettings.ini` (`%AppData%\Local\PhantomDoctrine`).

## Audio

Six sliders (music / SFX / voice, etc.).

**Difficulty-relevant tip:** incoming voice comms are quieter than outgoing -- lowering music/SFX volume aids hearing story cues.

## Interface

**Subtitles live here, NOT under Audio** -- a common place players look in the wrong tab.

## Gameplay

`Always Run`, `Show civilians on enemy bar`.

## Difficulty affecting perception

- Gamma (Video tab) -- brightens night-urban maps, most of the game.
- Music/SFX sliders (Audio tab) -- lowering aids hearing incoming voice comms.
- No colorblind-support, motion-blur, or controller-deadzone options surfaced by P1 research -- flag if found during play.
