## Weapon tiers

Weapons tier up in a fixed order per class; e.g. pistol order (per NamuWiki): **B76 -> Cobra -> .45 -> Wembley -> PPK -> TT.** Higher tiers appear as the story progresses, with top-tier (Level VI proficiency) weapons appearing just before the finale.

**Sources for weapons:** mission loot, containers, Vendor buy, Workshop craft (see `mechanics.md` Crafting/trading).

## Silencers / suppressors

Blueprint sourced from interrogating enemy agents or found in mission safes -> craft at Workshop or buy from Vendor. Usable only on proficient weapons (weapon proficiency training required). *Silent Power* achievement = mod a shotgun with a silencer.
