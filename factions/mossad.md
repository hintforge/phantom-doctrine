## Current status

One of the three playable starting agencies (alongside CIA and KGB). **Unlocks only after finishing one campaign** (NG+-style gate) -- not selectable on a first playthrough. Protagonist codename: **Hemlock** ("the Israeli Nazi Hunter").

## Recruitment / Access

Selected as the starting-agency choice, once unlocked. Recruits CIA agents more easily than KGB/Stasi-aligned agents (see `../mechanics.md` "Agency choice" for the full recruitment-affinity note).

## Capabilities

See `../paths/mossad.md` for the full path-specific breakdown (unique Chapter 1 missions, handler character, optional objectives).

## Quests

Chapter 1: chemical weapons lab, optional journalist rescue, smugglers' base (optional kill Ramses), intercept Komodo & Abaddon -- see `../nav/architecture.md` Chapter <-> Zone Mapping. Also grants an extra optional "Kill Abaddon" objective at the Chapter 3 houseboat mission.

## Achievements

*The Institute* (finish Ch1 Mossad) -- 4.81% Steam rarity, the rarest story achievement in the base list, since it requires a prior campaign clear first. See `../achievements.md` Branch section.
