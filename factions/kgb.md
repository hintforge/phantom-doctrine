## Current status

One of the three playable starting agencies (alongside CIA and Mossad). Available from campaign start (no unlock gate, unlike Mossad). Protagonist codename: **Kodiak.** Agents on this path speak Russian in voiceover.

## Recruitment / Access

Selected as the starting-agency choice at campaign start. Recruits Stasi/SB (communist-bloc) agencies' agents more easily than CIA/Mossad-aligned agents (see `../mechanics.md` "Agency choice" for the full recruitment-affinity note).
