# Path -- KGB

**status:** research-integrated
**last_reconciled:** 2026-08-18

Starting-agency branch. Protagonist codename **Kodiak.** See `../factions/kgb.md` for the organization-level entity file; this file covers the branch-specific playthrough content.

## What diverges from the other two paths

1. Entirely different Chapter 1 missions: arrest the traitor Yukon (found dead, follow blood trail to classified documents, investigate Vladivostok police), hideout raid, submarine sabotage (3 sabotage points; interlocks force combat after 2 turns -- **do sabotage points last**). Finishing = *Comrade Spy*.
2. Unique handler **Cyclone**, later revealed as a Beholder sleeper agent -- see `../npcs/cyclone.md`.
3. Russian voiceover -- a noted immersion feature.
4. Recruits Stasi/SB-aligned (communist-bloc) agents more easily than CIA/Mossad-aligned agents.

From Chapter 2 onward, converges with the other two paths into "The Cabal" -- no further path-specific content confirmed by P1 research past this point.
