## Current status

The player's own organization from Chapter 2 onward, once the CIA/KGB/Mossad storylines converge. Operates out of **The Hideout** (`hideout_hub`). See `../mechanics.md` "The Cabal -- roster and recruitment" for the full recruitment/cap/HQ-facility mechanics -- not duplicated here.

## Recruitment / Access

The organization the player builds via agent recruitment, HQ facility unlocks, and the Investigation Board. Max roster 35-40 agents depending on difficulty (see `../mechanics.md`).

## Capabilities

HQ facility screens (Crew Quarters, Comms, Forger, Infirmary, Workshop, Analytics, Body Engineering, MK-ULTRA) -- see `../mechanics.md` "HQ facility screens."

## Quests

The Cabal's internal security is threatened by **Undertow**, a Beholder mole embedded among the player's own agents -- see `../npcs/undertow.md`.

## Achievements

*Fully Operational* (unlock all facilities), *Well-equipped* (research full tech tree), *Top Tier* (agent to level 20), *Cold Truce* (mixed-agency squad) -- all organization-management achievements, see `../achievements.md`.
