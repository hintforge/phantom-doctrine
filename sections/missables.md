# Sections -- Missables

**status:** research-integrated
**last_reconciled:** 2026-08-18

Aggregated catalog of every missable item, achievement, collectible, or environmental detail in the game -- the cross-cutting summary a player consults to answer "what am I about to lose if I leave this section?" Sourced from `missable: yes` overlay tags across the corpus during P1 ingestion.

## Chapter 1 (faction-branched)

- **Faction achievements** (*Comrade Spy* / *US Intelligence* / *The Institute*) -- each Chapter-1 finale grants exactly one, tied to the starting-agency choice made at campaign start. Getting all three requires multiple playthroughs (and Mossad requires beating the game once first, so it can't be first). Not missable within a single playthrough -- it's a multi-playthrough completionist target.
- **Mossad-only optional objectives** (journalist rescue; optional kill Ramses in the smugglers' base +50 XP; 3 optional stealth objectives at the Komodo/Abaddon intercept, 33 XP each) -- available only on the Mossad starting-agency path, Chapter 1 only.

## Chapter 2

- ***Once Bitten, Twice Shy*** (hidden) -- must clear the Glasgow map and call evac BEFORE triggering the cargo pickup; interacting with the shipment forces combat. Chapter-bound -- missable if the sequence order is botched.

## Chapter 3

- ***No One Noticed*** -- Capture Tai-Pan without raising alarm. Needs Actor-perk disguised agents; Tai-Pan's corner position is static, so this mission should be planned last among the chapter's missions to maximize prep. Chapter-bound.
- ***Rock the Boat*** (hidden) / ***Same, but Different*** (Extended-campaign variant) -- Marquis-cargo recovery at the houseboat; the two achievements are mutually exclusive per save (regular vs Extended). Chapter-bound.

## Chapter 4

- ***Blessing in Disguise*** -- Rescue Boldface using a disguised agent specifically. **MISSABLE, Chapter 4 only** -- explicitly time-limited per the source research (not available to attempt after Ch4 closes).

## Chapter 5-6

- ***Explosive Measures*** (hidden, Ch5) / ***Death of a Citizen*** (Ch5, Extended-only) -- Broadsword-facility destruction vs. kill-in-Munich are mutually exclusive per save.
- ***Tip of the Iceberg*** (hidden, Ch6) -- only obtainable on a **regular** (non-Extended) playthrough; excluded entirely once the save is on the Extended campaign.

## Endgame / postgame

- **Chapter 8 (Extended campaign)** -- requires beating the game once (NG+ unlock); *The Whole Truth* achievement and the Ch8 `c8_killtaipan` mission only exist on Extended saves.
- **Mossad starting-agency path** -- unlocks only after beating the game once with CIA or KGB; not available on a first playthrough.
