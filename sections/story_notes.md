## Faction / character reveals (base-game scope, not tied to one specific mission beat)

- **Valhalla = Sven Galloway**, CEO of the Beholder Initiative (a US lobbying firm founded 1955 to perpetuate global conflict). Main antagonist. See `../npcs/valhalla.md`.
- **Cardinal = Valhalla's right-hand woman AND an undercover CIA mole** feeding the Cabal intel; she assassinated the previous Beholder CEO to install Valhalla. See `../npcs/cardinal.md`.
- **Undertow = a mole inside the Cabal** (a Beholder spy); must be executed to progress. **P2 resolved the discovery mechanism:** Undertow is a randomly-flagged existing roster agent, surfaced through strategic Hideout mole-hunt events (polygraph fail, illness/isolation, or drunk-spouse accusation), not a fixed pre-written character. See `../npcs/undertow.md` and `../mechanics.md` "Mole hunt (Undertow)".
- **Tai-Pan** = a minor Triad villain, captured/brainwashed in Chapter 3; in the Extended campaign's sequel hook he seizes Beholder remnants. See `../npcs/tai-pan.md`.
- **Project Iceberg** = Beholder's plan to decrypt/alter global communications in real time for societal control; ended when the Almaz Control Station is crashed to Earth. No entity overlay -- this is an operation/plan, not a person or faction.
