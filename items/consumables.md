## Ammunition mods (Workshop-craftable)

**Riot Slugs** (shotgun ammo mod) -- $540 to craft, 9h with the Crafting perk, tooltip reads Damage **-20**, Effect **Slowed**. Read as a control round: trades per-hit damage for slowing the target, for pinning a mobile target in place (about to flee, reach cover, or reach an objective/alarm) rather than dropping them outright -- a setup shot, not a damage pick. Also has resale value in the craft-for-profit tier (`../mechanics.md` "Early-game money" table): $755 sell, $215 profit, $16.54/hr baseline.

**Gap:** the exact mechanical size of "Slowed" (how much Movement Range it removes, how many turns it lasts) is not confirmed -- not in any tooltip captured yet. The purpose read above is inferred from the tooltip's own damage/effect tradeoff, not a sourced mechanic.
