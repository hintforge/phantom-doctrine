## Base relocation

The Hideout is not a fixed location. Danger accumulates toward a threshold; at the cap the base is raided (money lost, agents may be captured), then the player is prompted to relocate. **Relocation is not expensive -- you pay for how CLEAN the new hideout is, not for the move itself (player screenshot, 2026-09-16).** The Move Hideout screen offers three candidates at once, each with a cash cost and an Initial Danger bar that move together: observed live -- **Vienna $8000 (Initial Danger bar empty), Stockholm $4000 (slightly filled), Brussels $1000 (most filled)**. So ~$8000 buys a *pristine* base; a serviceable one starts around **$1000**. Never quote the $8000 figure as "the cost of relocating". Relocation is initiated from Analytics or by clicking a hideout candidate on the World Map. **Once abandoned, a hideout cannot be reused.** Hideout Concealment/Camouflage (from $750, 5h) and Radio Listening Post upgrades raise the danger threshold before a raid triggers.

**Danger spikes** from holding captured agents before Faraday Cages (Ch. 4) unlock, and from killing civilians. **This is NOT a soft-lock risk and should not be described as one (corrected 2026-09-16)** -- the cheapest relocation observed is $1000, so being unable to move at all would take near-total bankruptcy. The real trade is cash versus how much Danger you inherit at the new base: pay more for a cleaner start, or pay little and keep managing a part-full bar.

In the CIA campaign specifically, the early hideout is implied to be in Beirut -- a Ch1 story mission re-enters it to retrieve documents. `[Single source -- verify · class:wiki]`

## Common confusions

- "Danger is climbing and I don't know why" -- captured agents held before Ch4 Faraday Cages and killed civilians are the two biggest danger spikes. Check Analytics for the current danger bar.
- Players sometimes think the Hideout is one fixed place -- it is a persistent *role* in the zone graph, not a fixed location; it can and will relocate over a campaign.
