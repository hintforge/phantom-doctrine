**Correction to Stage-0 pre-research:** this game has **no discrete crafting-materials inventory.** Crafting (Workshop) consumes **cash + unlocked blueprints**, not a separate pool of gathered materials. The achievement names that suggested a crafting/trading economy at Stage 0 ("Tinker," "Merchant") are about item counts, not material resources -- see `../mechanics.md` "Crafting / trading" for the actual system.
